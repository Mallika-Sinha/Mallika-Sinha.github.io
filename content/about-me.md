Hey folks, thanks for visiting my page! I'm a PhD candidate in the School of Physics and Astronomy at Monash University 🐨. I love using some of the most energetic and voilent bangs in the Universe to learn more about extreme events that caused them. I do this by analysing gravitational waves detected by the the Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory ([[LIGO]](https://ligo.org/){:target="_blank"}). So far, we've detected gravitational waves from the collisions of black holes and neutron stars (both with themselves and with each other). A binary black hole merger can emit energy equivalent to the mass of several Suns in a fraction of a second! 

My work involves applying various data analysis techniques to gravitational-wave data in order to extract as much information we can out of them. Sometimes this means working to better understand the detectors we use to observe these gravitational waves and help shape them for the future. In particular, I've worked on:
* testing Einstein's general theory of relativity using black hole spectroscopy
* leveraging multi-messenger observations to learn more about neutron stars
* gravitational-wave detector calibration

I'm a member of the LIGO Scientific Collaboration and the ARC Centre of Excellence for Gravitational Wave Discovery ([[OzGrav]](https://www.ozgrav.org/#){:target="_blank"}).
